The needle finds north.

The mark turns solid and spins like a compass needle, settling to face you. The reflection fades, the peak steps into the word as its A, and the letters rise around it.

  1. 0.0–2.7sThe spinThe mark gains depth and spins horizontally like a solid compass needle, then settles facing forward and flattens.
  2. 2.7–3.4sThe reflection fadesThe lower half sinks a little and disappears.
  3. 3.3–4.4sThe peak becomes the AIt glides into place and narrows to the width of a letter.
  4. 3.9–5.1sThe letters riseFrom the baseline, spreading outward from the A.
