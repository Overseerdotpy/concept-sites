Northvale Expeditions · logo

The vale, then the peak.

The A in Northvale is a mountain. Set right after the V, the name dips into the valley and climbs to the summit, the whole journey the company guides you through, written into one word.

Primary

Wordmark

Barlow Condensed Bold, the Trail marker typeface, with the rust mountain as the A. It takes the exact space of a regular A, so the word reads as one even line of capitals, with the peak picked out in rust.

Variations

Reversed and one color

On granite or over photos, the letters turn snow-white and the mountain shifts to a lighter rust so it stays bright. Where only one color is possible, the mountain takes the letters' color and still reads by its shape.

Reversed, for dark backgrounds and photos

One color: stamps, embroidery, engraving

Standalone logo

The mountain and its reflection

A peak mirrored in a still alpine lake. Together the two halves form a compass needle, and like a real one, the red end points north: Northvale's north is the summit, its vale the valley lake below.

On light backgrounds the reflection is granite; on dark ones it turns snow-white so the lake still reads. The app icon sits on granite.

Rules

Using it

Clear space

Keep empty space around the wordmark at least the height of the mountain on every side.

Minimum size

The wordmark down to 18px tall, the app icon down to 16px.

Always capitals

NORTHVALE is always set in capitals in the logo. In sentences, write "Northvale".
